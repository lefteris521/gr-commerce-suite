Know which COD payments actually arrived

Couriers collect cash on delivery and pay it to you later in one transfer, with a statement that is hard to check by hand. The plugin matches each line of that statement to its voucher and order and shows every difference.

  • Exact to the cent, without rounding differences
  • Delivered parcels still unpaid after the agreed period
  • Accept a known difference and keep a record of it
  • Optional automatic check every day

Frequently asked questions

Which couriers are supported?

ACS Courier and BOX NOW. Geniki Taxydromiki and Speedex are in development and will be added as modules.

How does COD reconciliation find missing payments?

Couriers send a CSV with each COD payout. The plugin matches every line to its voucher and order and lists parcels that were delivered but not paid, paid short or missing from the statement.

Will the Skroutz feed slow down my store?

No. The feed is generated in small background batches instead of one long request, so large catalogues finish without server timeouts.

Is it compatible with HPOS?

Yes. GR Commerce Suite declares compatibility with WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS) and reads and writes order data only through the WooCommerce order API.

Does it work with the Blocks checkout?

Yes. Both the Blocks and the Classic checkout are supported, including the BOX NOW locker choice and the invoice or receipt choice.

Where is my data sent?

We run no servers that store your customer data. Plugin data stays in your WordPress database. Order details go only to services you switch on, such as your courier, AADE or Meta. The licence check sends only your licence key and site address.

What happens after the trial?

The licence continues at €89 per year for one site, with updates and support. The 14-day money-back guarantee applies from the first payment.

Can it handle large catalogues and many orders?

Yes. Feeds are built in batches of up to 200 products with saved checkpoints, COD statements are read line by line instead of loaded whole, and order lists are read from the database in limited batches.
